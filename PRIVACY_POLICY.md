# Privacy Policy for Reminda (Schedly)

**Last Updated:** August 28, 2026

Reminda ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information and timetable data are collected, used, and safeguarded when using our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
When signing in with Google or creating an account via email:
- Your name, email address, and authentication identifiers are stored to facilitate cloud synchronization.
- Guest mode users can use the app anonymously without providing an email address.

### B. Schedule & Timetable Data
- Schedule entries created manually or extracted via camera scanning (e.g., subject titles, shift timings, days of the week, classroom or workplace locations, and instructor/supervisor notes).
- Schedule profile configurations (e.g. School, Hospital Duty, Work Shift, Government).

### C. Device & Operational Preferences
- Notification preferences, custom alarm ringtone selections, and reminder lead times.
- Feedback messages and ratings submitted via the In-App Feedback system.

---

## 2. On-Device AI & OCR Privacy

- **Offline Processing:** Images and PDF documents scanned in Offline Mode are processed 100% locally on your device using Google ML Kit Vision. No images or scanned schedule photos are sold to advertising networks.
- **Online Cloud AI Enhancement:** When choosing to use online AI features (Google Gemini Vision or OpenRouter), schedule images are transmitted over secure TLS encrypted connections strictly for timetable extraction and are not retained for marketing.
- **Anonymous Dataset Improvement:** Users can optionally choose to help improve offline AI accuracy by contributing anonymous layout formatting. Personal identifiers (such as student IDs, phone numbers, and home addresses) are strictly excluded, and this setting can be toggled off at any time in AI Settings.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage:** Your schedule data is stored locally on your device using encrypted Hive key-value storage.
- **Cloud Database:** When logged in with a Google account, your data is securely synced with your private Google Firebase Firestore cloud database, protected by granular security rules ensuring only your authenticated account can access your timetable.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Reminda utilizes industry-leading infrastructure to provide secure services:
- **Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore** (Account management & cloud backup)
- **Google ML Kit Vision** (On-device text extraction)
- **Google Gemini API** (Optional online schedule parsing)

These services are bound by Google's strict privacy and security standards.

---

## 5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain complete ownership of your data at all times:
- **Export Data:** You can export your schedules at any time in formatted text, CSV, or JSON backup.
- **Permanently Delete:** You can permanently delete individual schedules, whole profiles, or your entire account and all associated cloud data with one tap from the Profile screen.
